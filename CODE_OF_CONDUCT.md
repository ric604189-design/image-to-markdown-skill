# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and considerate.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus discussion on the work and its evidence.
- Protect private documents, credentials, and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of
private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the route in SECURITY.md.
Maintainers will review reports promptly and may remove content or
participation when necessary to protect the community.
